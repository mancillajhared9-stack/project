About Us

The Family Planning Organization of the Philippines (FPOP) is a non-profit organization established in 1969 to champion reproductive rights and sexual health.

Our Vision

A Filipino society where every person has full access to quality sexual and reproductive health information and services.

Our Mission

To empower individuals, families, and communities through comprehensive health services, outreach, and policy advocacy.
